Drop Edition is the co-creation of Jieun Kim and Mimi Kim, two entrepreneurial women with careers in fashion spanning from Seoul to Tokyo, to LA and NYC. Drop Edition is a Smart contemporary clothing designed for your goals. Versatile pieces, compact collection, endless style possibilities.

Drop Edition will be contributing a portion of each sale to non-for-profit organizations and support with the community. We believe if you look your best, it's a small but important step towards becoming best version of yourself.
